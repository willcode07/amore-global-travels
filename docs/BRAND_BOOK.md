Prepared for Amore Global Travels · Johnson Unlimited LLC

## 2. Personality

If Amore were a person, they would be:

- Welcoming and calm  
- Detail-oriented without being stiff  
- Proud of the journey, not just the booking  
- Family-minded and respectful  

They would **not** be pushy, flashy for its own sake, or hard to reach.

## 3. Logo

### Primary mark
Heart-shaped globe + **Amore Global** wordmark (script “Amore” + serif “Global”).

**Files**
- Light surfaces: `/images/logo.png`
- Dark surfaces: `/images/logo-alt.png`
- Favicon: gold heart only (`/favicon.svg` and `src/app/icon.svg`) — no underline under the icon

### Clear space
Leave clear space around the logo at least equal to the height of the heart icon.

### Minimum size
- Digital: heart icon no smaller than **32px** tall  
- Prefer icon + wordmark together in the header  

### Do
- Use on cream, white, soft photo overlays, or deep ink  
- Keep proportions locked  
- Switch to the alt logo on dark bands and in dark mode  

### Don’t
- Stretch, outline, recolor randomly, or place on busy photo without a soft scrim  
- Add drop shadows or glow effects  
- Put a line or slogan under the favicon  

**Logo change is out of scope.** Keep the existing mark.

## 4. Color palette

Warm cream + gold is the Amore signature. Use color sparingly so gold stays special.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Sand | `#F7F3EB` | Page background (light) |
| Surface | Ivory | `#FFFFFF` | Forms, panels |
| Soft field | Cream | `#FBF3E4` | Section bands |
| Ink | Deep Olive | `#171C19` | Headlines, body, footer |
| Muted | Sage Gray | `#5E6762` | Supporting text |
| Line | Soft Stone | `#E4DDD0` | Borders, dividers |
| Accent | Amore Gold | `#E8920C` | Primary buttons, key accents |
| Accent deep | Bronze | `#7A5600` | Links, labels, hover states |
| Inverse | Night | `#171C19` | Footer, dark bands |

**Dark mode:** Follow the visitor’s browser theme. Invert page chrome; keep gold; keep the footer ink. Written quotes stay cream even in dark mode (they are print documents).

**Rule of thumb:** One gold action per section. Cream for calm. Ink for words.

---

## 5. Typography

| Role | Family | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display / headings | **Fraunces** | 550–600 | Elegant, readable; not decorative script in long paragraphs |
| Body / UI | **Figtree** | 400–600 | Clear for forms, nav, and buttons |
| Labels | Figtree | 600 | Uppercase + wide tracking for section eyebrows only |

Avoid more than two type families on any screen.

## 6. Voice & messaging

### Sound like
- “We’ll research options and share them in your trip dashboard.”  
- “Call us — we’re glad to help.”  
- “Nothing books until you say so.”  

### Avoid
- “Unmissable deals!!!”  
- Self-serve checkout language (“Select yacht”, “Make payment”, “Book now” as a shopping cart)  
- Fake guest names or invented review counts  

**Public copy note:** Marketing text can still be refined when Amore delivers final wording. Do not invent testimonials.

---

## 7. Imagery

### Prefer
- Real destinations, ships, ports, and travelers in natural light  
- Wide, calm compositions that feel like a trip, not a stock catalog  
- Warm light that matches cream + gold  

### Avoid
- Neon filters, purple glow, cluttered collages  
- Fake handshake “business travel” clichés  

### Hero rule
Full-bleed image. Brand first. One headline. One short line. One clear CTA group.

## 8. UI patterns

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Buttons (primary) | Gold fill, dark text, pill shape, soft hover darken |
| Buttons (secondary) | Light border on photo or line border on cream |
| Forms | Surface fields, soft stone borders, gold focus ring |
| Sections | Generous space; one job per section |
| Motion | Soft fade / gentle rise; never distracting |
| Quotes | Cream document, gold stay header, ink totals — print-ready |

## 11. Product rules (from kickoff)

- Agent-led quotes only. No traveler self-booking plugins in this phase.  
- Traveler login is **email + phone**, one dashboard for every trip.  
- Agent inbox stays **one row per trip**.  
- Every traveler message emails the other party (revisit if it gets noisy).  
- Vacation content is regional and evergreen. Specific packages can be added later without redesigning the page.
